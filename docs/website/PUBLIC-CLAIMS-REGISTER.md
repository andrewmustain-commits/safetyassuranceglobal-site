## Release Rule

A claim appearing in repository source is not, by itself, evidence that the claim is true or currently authorized for expansion. Material claims must have a traceable evidence source, an accountable owner, and a current review disposition before they are introduced, materially changed, or amplified.

Where current authoritative evidence is unavailable, the release process must fail closed: preserve an already-controlled representation only when its existing release authority remains applicable, otherwise hold the new or changed claim until evidence and approval are recorded.

## Institute Boundary

The corporate site may identify and link to the Safety Assurance Global Institute of Assurance using approved public information. It must not independently convert Institute architecture, course records, pilots, governance artifacts, or internal readiness work into claims of public enrollment, accreditation, regulatory approval, recognition, credential issuance, certificate issuance, or public verification.

## Change Control

Updates to this register require repository review. Legal/regulatory-sensitive claims, government qualification/status claims, accreditation/recognition claims, and production-capability claims require evidence appropriate to the claim before public expansion.
