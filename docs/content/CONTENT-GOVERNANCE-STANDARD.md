## Executive Summary
This document is the highest-authority governance standard for the Safety Assurance Global internal knowledge library under `/docs/content/`. It defines how content is created, reviewed, approved, maintained, and archived using controlled processes that protect accuracy, legal safety, and brand consistency.

## Purpose
The purpose of this standard is to establish mandatory governance controls for internal Markdown content documents and content operations. It ensures content remains auditable, consistent, and aligned with organizational requirements.

## Scope
This standard applies to:
- All internal governance documents in `/docs/content/`
- All draft and approved knowledge articles managed in repository content locations
- All contributors, reviewers, and approvers participating in the content lifecycle

This standard does not authorize direct publication actions or deployment overrides.

## Governance Roles
- Author: Drafts and updates content according to all governed standards.
- Technical Reviewer: Verifies technical accuracy, completeness, and traceability.
- Legal/Regulatory Reviewer: Reviews compliance-sensitive content when required.
- Executive Reviewer: Final approval authority before repository publication workflow steps.
- Repository Maintainer: Ensures process compliance, version control integrity, and auditability.

## Document Status Definitions
- Draft: Working state; not approved for publication.
- Review: Under formal review by designated reviewers.
- Approved: Authorized for publication workflow progression.
- Published: Live in approved publishing channel after all controls pass.
- Periodic Review: Scheduled reassessment state for published content.
- Updated: Approved post-publication revision state.
- Archived: Retained for record; no longer active.

## Content Hierarchy
1. Governance Standards (policy-level controls)
2. Operational Standards (workflow and metadata controls)
3. Templates and Checklists (execution aids)
4. Content Artifacts (articles and related assets)
5. Logs and Calendars (planning and traceability)

## Success Metrics
- 100% of governed content includes complete required metadata.
- 100% of published content has documented executive approval.
- 0 known fabricated citations, regulations, or unsupported claims.
- 100% of reviewed content passes checklist controls prior to approval.
- Review cadence compliance at or above 95% per quarter.

## Review Cadence
- Scheduled governance review: Quarterly
- Minimum formal review points: Effective date cycle and next review cycle
- Triggered review events:
	- Regulatory or legal change
	- Brand or trademark updates
	- Material workflow or schema changes
	- Incident findings related to content quality or compliance

## Exceptions
Exceptions must be:
- Documented in writing with rationale and impact assessment
- Approved by Executive Reviewer before implementation
- Time-bounded with expiration or reassessment date
- Logged in `CHANGELOG.md` and referenced in impacted documents

No exception may bypass legal obligations or executive approval requirements.

## Change Control
- All updates require a pull request with clear summary and rationale.
- Governance-impacting changes require Executive Reviewer approval.
- Version updates must follow semantic versioning for governance docs.
- Every approved change must be recorded in `CHANGELOG.md`.
- Breaking governance changes require linked updates to dependent standards.
