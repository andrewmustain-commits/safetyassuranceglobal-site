## Purpose
This index is the navigation map for the governed content library in `/docs/content/`. It identifies authority, dependencies, and reading sequence so contributors and reviewers can apply standards in the correct order.

## Dependencies
Core dependency model:
- `CONTENT-GOVERNANCE-STANDARD.md` is the controlling dependency for all governance documents.
- `METADATA-STANDARD.md` and `CONTROLLED-VOCABULARY.md` define required field and taxonomy constraints used by templates and review.
- `BLOG-STYLE-GUIDE.md` depends on governance, metadata, and vocabulary standards.
- `ARTICLE-TEMPLATE.md` depends on metadata and style standards.
- `PUBLICATION-WORKFLOW.md` depends on governance and review standards.
- `AI-CONTENT-GOVERNANCE.md` depends on governance and workflow controls.
- `REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md` depends on all applicable standards and workflow stages.
- `EDITORIAL-CALENDAR.md` depends on vocabulary and workflow statuses.
- `CHANGELOG.md` depends on approved governance change control.
- `README.md` provides orientation and entry context for this index.
