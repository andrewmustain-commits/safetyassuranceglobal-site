Mobilization readiness can fail when execution teams rely on assumptions instead of verified evidence.

## Verify critical controls before work starts

Confirm that high-consequence controls are defined, assigned, and understood by both direct teams and contractors.

## Validate contractor oversight readiness

Ensure contractor qualification checks, QA/QC checkpoints, and escalation pathways are active before mobilization starts.

## Confirm response and escalation pathways

Verify that leaders can rapidly escalate hazards, evidence gaps, and control failures without delay.
